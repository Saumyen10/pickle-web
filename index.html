Our story

Small kitchen, bright flavours.

Every pickle journey starts the same way it always has — raw materials dried in sunlight, spices pounded/mixed by hand, mustard oil and patience doing the rest. No factory, no shortcuts, and in some weeks we simply sell out.

A beautiful treasury of Assamese culture and taste!
From pickles, Mekhela-Chador, and Gamosa to authentic homemade snacks like Pithaguri and Sandah, everything is available with us throughout the season.

Our pickles

Aam

Raw mangoes blended with mustard oil and five-spice, traditionally pickled for a bold, aromatic flavour.

Amlokhi

Tangy amla pickled with mustard oil and roasted spices for a sharp, sour and flavourful bite.

Baah-gos (DRY)

Baah-gos

বাঁহগজৰ আচাৰ

Fermented bamboo shoot, slow-pickled in mustard oil and spices for a distinctive tangy-spicy flavour.

Bhoot-jolokia

Assam's world-famous Bhoot Jolokia, hand-pickled into a fiery achar — only for those who dare.

Authentic Bhoot Jolokia paste, finely ground in a mixer grinder — just a little can add a powerful kick to your food!

Kordoi

Whole sour star fruit pickled in mustard oil and spices for a tangy, savoury treat.

Ou-tenga

ওউটেঙাৰ আচাৰ

Nemu

নেমুৰ আচাৰ

A fragrant homemade pickle made by rubbing salt onto sun-dried big round lemons and letting them slowly mature in the natural juice formed as the salt dissolves.

Jolphai

Mix

A wonderful blend of several pickles prepared with homemade spices — perfect for those who want to enjoy a little of every flavour in one pickle.

Beyond pickles

More from the kitchen

Our shop isn't just pickles (achar) — these change with the season and demand, so ask in-store for what's fresh today.

Pithaguri

পিঠাগুড়ি

Traditionally prepared rice flour made by soaking, drying, and finely grinding rice — perfect for making homemade pitha, laru and to eat with jalpan.

Sandoh

সান্দহ

Prepared by soaking and dry-roasting rice until golden colour arises, then grinding it into a fragrant powder — typically enjoyed as jalpan with milk and jaggery.

Kharoli (Behar)

খাৰলি (বেহুৱা)

A homemade dish rolled in roasted mustard powder and freshly wrapped in banana leaf — mildly spicy with a distinctive flavour.

Kola-khar

কলাখাৰ

Made by filtering the ash of sun-dried banana peels — an essential ingredient for preparing khar dishes at home.
