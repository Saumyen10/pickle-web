Our story

A family kitchen, not a factory

Maa has been making pickles the same way for as long as anyone in the family can remember — chillies sun-dried on the roof, spices hand-pounded, every jar aged in mustard oil until the flavour is just right. Nothing here is mass-produced, and some weeks we simply run out.

We sell handmade Assamese pickles including bamboo shoot, olive, mango and lemon. Also selling traditional gamusa, Asomiya sadar mekhela clothes, and raw materials for Assamese jalpan such as sandah, laru and pitha. ইয়াতে হাতে তৈয়াৰ কৰা অসমীয়া আচাৰ উপলব্ধ — বাঁহগাজ, জলফাই, আম আৰু নেমুৰ আচাৰ আদি। ইয়াৰ লগতে পৰম্পৰাগত গামোচা, অসমীয়া চাদৰ-মেখেলা কাপোৰ আৰু অসমীয়া জলপান - সানদহ, লাৰু আৰু পিঠা উপলব্ধ।
